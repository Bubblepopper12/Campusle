Campusle: the daily college guessing game

Guess the mystery college in six tries. Every guess shows clues comparing your pick to the answer: distance and direction on the map, enrollment, acceptance rate, tuition, founding year and athletic conference. A new puzzle every day at midnight, the same for everyone. Free, no account needed.

Four daily games

  • Classic: well-known U.S. colleges and universities.
  • Classic · Hard: niche schools, from small liberal arts colleges to HBCUs and engineering schools.
  • Football: FBS college football programs, with stadium, conference, titles and team-color clues.
  • Players: famous college football players, with school, position, Heisman, titles and jersey clues.
