How to Play Campusle

Rules, clue colors and strategy tips

The basics

  1. Every day there is one mystery answer in each mode. Everyone playing that mode gets the same answer.
  2. Type a guess in the search box. It suggests matches as you type, and it understands nicknames and abbreviations such as "UT", "A&M", "Bama" or "Johnny Football".
  3. Each guess adds a row of six clue tiles comparing your guess to the answer.
  4. You have six guesses. Use the clues to narrow it down. A new puzzle starts at midnight where you live.

What the colors mean

  • Green: an exact match or very close (within 5%). For location, green means the same state.
  • Yellow: close (within 25%), or within 250 miles on the map.
  • Orange: on the map only, within 750 miles.
  • Red: on the map only, more than 750 miles away.
  • Gray: further off. An arrow ⬆ means the answer is higher; ⬇ means it is lower.

Years such as the founding date are judged against the answer's age, so being five years off on a school founded in 1880 counts as close, while being ninety years off does not.

Classic and Hard clues

  • 📍 Where: the distance in miles from your guess to the answer, with an arrow pointing toward the answer on the map.
  • 👥 Size: undergraduate enrollment.
  • 🎟️ Admit: the acceptance rate.
  • 💵 Tuition: in-state tuition and fees for one year.
  • 🏛️ Founded: the year the school was founded.
  • 🏆 Conf: the athletic conference. Schools outside Division I show Division II, Division III, NAIA or None.

Tip: start with a big, central school such as a large state flagship. The distance arrow and the enrollment arrow together usually cut the map down fast.

Football clues

  • 📍 Where: distance and direction, the same as Classic.
  • 🏆 Conf: the program's football conference this season.
  • 🏟️ Stadium: home stadium capacity.
  • 📜 Since: the first season of varsity football.
  • 🥇 Titles: national championships the school claims (yellow means within two).
  • 🎨 Colors: team colors. Green means the same main color; yellow means at least one shared color (white does not count).

Players clues

  • 🏫 School: the player's main college. Yellow means they also played there (transfers count) or the schools are in the same conference today.
  • 🏈 Pos: position. Yellow means the same side of the ball.
  • 📅 Final: the player's final college season (yellow means within three years).
  • 🏆 Heisman: whether they won the Heisman Trophy.
  • 💍 Titles: national championships their team won while they were on the roster (yellow means within one).
  • #️⃣ Jersey: their best-known college jersey number (yellow means within five).

Sharing and stats

When you finish, the Share button copies an emoji grid of your clue colors, with no names, so you can post it without spoiling the answer. Each mode keeps its own games played, win percentage, current streak, best streak and guess distribution on your device.
