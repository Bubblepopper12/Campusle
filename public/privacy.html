Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Campusle is a free daily guessing game. There are no accounts and we don't ask for your name, email or any other personal information.

What Campusle stores on your device

Your guesses, game progress and stats are saved in your browser's local storage so they survive a refresh. This data stays on your device. It is never sent to us, and you can clear it any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Advertising

Campusle shows ads served by Google AdSense to keep the game free. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance.

  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings, or opt out of third-party personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and secure the service.

Children

Campusle is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
